1. Overview

This Privacy Policy applies to Brainrot Clicker, its official website, and related services operated by FR1EV Studios ("we," "us," or "our").

Brainrot Clicker is currently designed to function without requiring a FR1EV Studios account. We aim to collect as little personal information as reasonably necessary.

No FR1EV account required You do not need to create a separate account with us to play.
No advertising tracking The current version does not contain targeted advertising SDKs.
Local save data Game progress and settings are primarily stored on your device.
Optional platform features Steam and Discord may process information under their own privacy policies.

2. Information we collect

Information collected directly

We may receive information that you voluntarily provide, such as:

  • your email address and the contents of messages you send to support;
  • information you post in our official Discord community;
  • screenshots, save files, diagnostic logs, or device information you choose to send when requesting help;
  • feedback, bug reports, reviews, survey answers, or other communications.

Information collected automatically by the Game

The current version of Brainrot Clicker does not operate its own advertising, behavioral tracking, or analytics system.

The Game may create technical files on your device, including save data, configuration files, and diagnostic logs. These files remain on your device unless you submit them to us or a platform synchronizes them.

Update this section before adding analytics, crash-reporting services, online accounts, cloud databases, advertisements, multiplayer servers, or payment systems operated directly by FR1EV Studios.

3. Local game and diagnostic data

Brainrot Clicker may store the following information locally on your computer:

  • game progress and upgrade levels;
  • settings such as sound, display, and language choices;
  • achievement or progression state;
  • temporary cache or technical configuration data;
  • local diagnostic logs used to identify technical problems.

Local data is generally controlled through your device. It may be deleted by uninstalling the Game, deleting its save folder, or using an in-game reset option where available.

On Windows, some Godot-based local data may be stored in a folder similar to:

%APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\brainrot clicker\

4. Discord Rich Presence

Brainrot Clicker uses the Discord Social SDK to provide optional Rich Presence features when the Discord desktop application is running.

Rich Presence may display information on your Discord profile such as:

  • that you are playing Brainrot Clicker;
  • your current general activity or game state;
  • how long the current play session has lasted;
  • game artwork;
  • buttons linking to the official server or store page.

The current implementation does not use Discord account linking, Discord authentication, private messages, voice chat, friends-list access, or in-game Discord communication.

Discord processes information according to its own privacy policy and account settings. You can control activity sharing through Discord's privacy settings or by closing Discord.

5. Steam and platform services

If you obtain or play the Game through Steam, Valve may process information such as:

  • your Steam account and Steam ID;
  • purchase and license information;
  • playtime and achievement information;
  • Steam Cloud save information, if enabled;
  • device, technical, security, and usage information.

Payment information is processed by Steam or another store, not directly by FR1EV Studios. We do not receive your full payment-card number from Steam.

Steam's own terms and privacy policy govern its processing of your information.

6. Website, server logs, and contact forms

If you visit an official FR1EV Studios website, the hosting provider may automatically process ordinary technical information needed to deliver and secure the site, including:

  • IP address;
  • browser and device type;
  • requested pages and access time;
  • referring page;
  • security, error, and server logs.

If the website later uses cookies, analytics, advertising, embedded videos, or additional tracking technologies, this Privacy Policy and any required consent controls must be updated.

7. Why we use information

We may process information for the following purposes:

  • to operate and maintain the Game;
  • to save settings and progression;
  • to provide Discord Rich Presence;
  • to respond to support requests and feedback;
  • to diagnose crashes, bugs, or compatibility issues;
  • to protect the Game, users, and services from abuse;
  • to comply with legal obligations;
  • to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

Where applicable, our legal grounds may include performing a contract with you, our legitimate interests in operating and securing the Game, your consent, and compliance with legal obligations.

8. When information may be shared

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be shared only where reasonably necessary with:

  • platform providers such as Valve/Steam and Discord;
  • website-hosting, email, security, or technical service providers;
  • professional advisers such as accountants or lawyers;
  • government authorities where disclosure is legally required;
  • a successor organisation in connection with a merger, acquisition, restructuring, or transfer of the Game.

Service providers are expected to process information only for appropriate purposes and to protect it according to applicable law.

9. Data retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including legal, security, accounting, and dispute-resolution requirements.

Examples may include:

  • support communications retained while a request is active and for a reasonable period afterward;
  • security records retained for as long as reasonably necessary to investigate abuse;
  • records retained where required by tax, accounting, or other laws.

Locally stored save data remains on your device until you remove it, reset the Game, or a platform deletes synchronized copies.

10. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

No internet service, device, or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You should protect your Steam, Discord, email, and device credentials.

11. Children's privacy

Brainrot Clicker is not intended to knowingly collect personal information directly from children under the minimum age required by applicable law without the necessary parental authorization.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information directly to FR1EV Studios, contact us so that we can investigate and, where appropriate, delete it.

Third-party services such as Steam and Discord apply their own age requirements and parental controls.

12. Your privacy rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights concerning your personal information, including the right to:

  • request access to information we hold about you;
  • request correction of inaccurate information;
  • request deletion in appropriate circumstances;
  • object to or restrict certain processing;
  • withdraw consent where processing relies on consent;
  • request a portable copy of certain information;
  • complain to your local data-protection authority.

To make a request, contact us using the information below. We may need to confirm your identity before completing the request.

Requests concerning information held independently by Steam or Discord must generally be sent directly to those companies.

13. International data transfers

Steam, Discord, hosting providers, and other service providers may process information in countries outside your country of residence.

Where required by law, appropriate protections will be used for international transfers, such as approved contractual safeguards or recognised adequacy decisions.
